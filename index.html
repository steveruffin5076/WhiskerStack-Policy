Whisker Stack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Whisker Stack mobile game ("the game", package name com.whiskerstack.game) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is published by the Whisker Stack developer ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

2. Game progress stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, cats, and home items) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads, through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads viewed and clicked
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data

This data is collected and used by Google, not by us, under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your Android settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path depends on your device). Where required by law, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

4. Permissions

The game uses internet access and the advertising ID permission only to load and measure ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or precise location.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google AdMob, as described in section 3.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account or Android settings.

7. Security

Data sent between the game and Google AdMob is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
